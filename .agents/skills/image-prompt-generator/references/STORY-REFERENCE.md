# Story Continuity: Alta - The Lost Sky Pirate Returns to Treasure Island

Read this when the brief names a character, faction, location, object, relationship, or story state. Use named continuity to ground the design. Keep all story-specific proper names out of image prompts.

## Premise

Mouse, an orphaned factory worker, discovers a map to the Floating Island of Silver and joins the feared sky-pirate captain Long John Silver in a search for lost family legacy. The adventure brings factory power, piracy, hidden inheritance, mechanical warfare, and ancient blue energy into conflict.

## Role Profiles

- **Jim Silver / Mouse, 18:** blond hair, blue eyes, factory laborer. Brave, curious, and underestimated. Carries a concealed ship's-wheel birthmark that releases protective blue energy. Early designs use practical work clothes.
- **Captain Long John Silver, 45-50:** formerly Lord John Silver, heir to the House of Silver and Jim's father. Scarred, bearded, one eye covered by an eyepatch. One leg is a steam-powered prosthetic; one arm is brass and steel with a claw that can deploy a sword, grappling hook, or rocket thruster. Carries a steam-powered laser gun. Charismatic, tactical, and hardened by loss.
- **Mad Maggie, 22:** blond, tattooed arms, blade fighter. Fearless, guarded, and intimidating. Wears practical pirate combat layers.
- **Victor Grimshaw, 50s:** ruthless owner of the Grimshaw factories. Visually controlled, imposing, and materially privileged relative to laborers.
- **Kip Grimshaw, 22:** Victor's spoiled, cowardly son and Mouse's bully. Dresses in inherited luxury that signals unearned privilege.
- **Blacktooth and Finch:** Long John's dangerous working-pirate crew. Blacktooth is a hulking brute; Finch is a knife-wielding opportunist. Give each a distinct silhouette and equipment.

## Places and Assets

- **Steam City:** sprawling steampunk metropolis of brick smokestacks, square metal factories, furnaces, pistons, steam, smog, docks, and oppressive industry. Skyships move above the city.
- **Grimshaw Factory:** hot and smoky, with giant furnaces and steam engines. It conveys class exploitation and mechanical danger.
- **Floating Island of Silver:** legendary airborne destination. It carries mystery, lost noble heritage, and aerial geography; define its exact architecture when the brief needs it.
- **The Storm Runner:** Long John's wooden skyship with three masts, flowing white sails, brass propellers beneath the stern, a main deck, captain's cabin, and weathered pirate use. It reads as a real sailing vessel made airborne.
- **Silver puzzle box:** compact object with silver-toned seams, gears, strange engravings, and a ship's-wheel emblem. It projects a blue holographic map of cloud routes, floating islands, and a silver X.

## Visual Alignments

- **House of Silver:** fallen royal skyfaring legacy with ship's-wheel emblems, silver accents, blue-white sails or energy, maintained craftsmanship, and lost history.
- **Grimshaw industrial empire:** dark practical metal, smoke, brick, furnace heat, red searchlights, and coercive power.
- **Long John's crew:** repaired wood, sailcloth, brass fittings, individual pirate wear, practical weapons, and long-term shipboard use.
- **Dreadnought automatons:** Grimshaw-aligned industrial war machines with brass armor, iron rivets, red glowing eyes, red searchlights, and a heavy fifteen-foot scale.

## Continuity Cues

- Early story state: Mouse and Long John remain unaware that they are father and son.
- Early Mouse designs emphasize working-class identity; House of Silver status and insignia emerge later in the story.
- The birthmark follows a ship's-wheel geometry.
- Royal energy is blue; industrial surveillance and threat lighting are red.
- Steam technology and supernatural blue energy remain visually distinct while interacting in the same world.
