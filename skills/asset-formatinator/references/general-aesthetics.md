# Project Style Guide: Alta - The Lost Sky Pirate Returns to Treasure Island

## Scope

Apply this guide to every Alta asset. It supplies visual language only. The brief supplies the requested subject and function; `scripts/STORY-REFERENCE.md` supplies named-story continuity; generator references supply prompt construction.

## World

This is a Victorian fantasy-steampunk pirate adventure with the color, theatrical clarity, and handcrafted wonder of a storybook fantasy film. Its world is divided between a steam-powered industrial city and an open sky of flying ships, floating islands, piracy, lost inheritance, and concealed ancient power.

Technology is visible, mechanical, late-19th-century in feel, and physically legible: boilers, piston rods, pressure pipes, valves, exposed rivets, geared housings, mechanical controls, brass, copper, iron, steel, timber, canvas, leather, and glass. Treat every fantastical element as a crafted object with clear construction, material, ownership, and purpose.

## Core Visual Contrast

- **Industrial authority:** brick, iron, furnace heat, sharp industrial geometry, engineered precision, and imposing scale. Its charcoal, red, brass, and furnace-orange palette feels forceful.
- **Royal skyfaring legacy:** blue-and-white sailcloth, silver details, refined timber, noble maintenance, a ship's-wheel emblem, open cloudscape, wind, and adventurous scale. Its palette centers on clean blue, cloud white, sail cream, silver, and brass gold.
- **Piracy:** finished timber, patterned mixed sailcloth, brass hardware, nautical rigging, customized fittings, reclaimed components recast into personal equipment, and individual crew insignia. Their ships and clothing combine maritime craft with precise engineering, storybook finery, decorative tailoring, and distinctive personal equipment. Their palette spans the full storybook spectrum: blues, greens, reds, crimsons, purples, golds, ochres, pinks, creams, and warm leathers, held together by brass and copper hardware. Combine several colors within each pirate design through patterned textiles, embroidered panels, contrasting linings, sashes, trim, and painted equipment.
- **Ancient royal power:** blue energy, blue-white holographic linework, a ship's-wheel emblem, precise geometry, and ceremonial color contrast.

Let the contrast create hierarchy through ornament, material care, magic, and saturation according to place and ownership. Keep all surfaces clean, finished, and carefully maintained.

## Skyship Rules

Skyships are sailing vessels adapted for flight. Preserve wooden hulls, decks, masts, spars, rigging, sails, and nautical proportions. Integrate propulsion, pressure, and lifting systems into that maritime structure. Keep the hull, masts, and sail plan visually dominant.

For royal craft, favor blue-and-white sailcloth, silver details, noble maintenance, and a recognizable ship's-wheel emblem when requested. For pirate craft, favor maintained timber, patterned mixed sailcloth, custom fittings, individualized deck equipment, and purposeful armament with varied wood, canvas, leather, and metal tones.

## Steampunk Discipline

Use mechanical details where they communicate function, craft history, ownership, or scale. Gears sit inside or near a visible mechanism; pipes connect to a source and destination; pistons drive an articulated part; rivets join plates or braces. Favor selective, functional hardware and a varied material palette.

## Materials and Color

Use materially specific surfaces: finished oak, braided rope, waxed canvas, polished brass, polished copper, lacquered iron, brushed steel, enamel paint, clean brick, leather, painted sailcloth, colored glass, and furnace glass.

Favor rich storybook color anchored in believable materials. Apply the faction relationships established in `Core Visual Contrast` through textiles, paint, glass, enamel, timber, and metal. Use color to clarify ownership, class, location, function, and emotional temperature.

## Integrated Mechanical Elements

Mechanical limbs, weapons, machinery, and automatons follow the same functional discipline as the rest of the world. Show how they attach, articulate, carry load, transfer force, and receive power. Use `character-generator.md` for character integration, `prop-generator.md` for object and machine construction, and `scripts/STORY-REFERENCE.md` for established identity.

## Cinematic Finish

Target a high-end live-action storybook fantasy-adventure finish: real materials, coherent scale, natural faces and anatomy, and physically integrated cloud, steam, and energy effects. Favor clear readable forms, expressive color contrast, crafted surfaces, theatrical but motivated lighting, and a sense of discovery.
