## Series Premise

Steam City is an industrial metropolis beneath skies crossed by steam-powered sailing vessels. Jim Silver, known as Mouse,
has spent fifteen years as an exploited factory worker after being separated from his father during the destruction of the
House of Silver. A silver puzzle box responds to his ship-wheel birthmark, reveals a map to the legendary Floating Island of
Silver, and awakens protective blue energy within him. Mouse joins the feared sky-pirate captain Long John Silver, unaware
that Long John is his father. Victor and Kip Grimshaw accompany them while secretly planning to seize the treasure. Through
the first twenty episodes, Mouse earns a place aboard the Storm Runner, develops a bond with Long John and Mad Maggie,
survives a lightning storm and a floating ship graveyard, and begins discovering his inherited abilities. The arc ends as the
Floating Island of Silver appears through the clouds; Mouse and Long John still have not confirmed their relationship.

## Complete Episode Continuity

### Episode 1 - Fall of the House of Silver

- Steam City is introduced as a sprawling steampunk metropolis of brick smokestacks, square metal factories, exposed brass
  gears, pumping pistons, hissing steam, black smoke, and airborne sailing ships rebuilt with steam technology.
- A bright blue-and-white royal airship descends with its sails burning and crashes through buildings into a city street.
  Fire consumes the wreckage; timber splinters and metal twists across the crash site.
- Lord Jonathan Silver, in his 30s, emerges badly wounded. His arm bleeds and one leg drags, but both limbs are still natural.
  He retrieves his terrified three-year-old son Jim from the wreckage and wraps him in a blanket.
- Two smaller, faster black warships descend through the smoke. Mechanical hatches beneath their sterns release two
  fifteen-foot Dreadnought automatons with brass armor, iron rivets, glowing red eyes, and sweeping red searchlights.
- John carries Jim into a nearby alley while the automatons pursue with ground-shaking steps. He hides Jim among discarded
  gears and scrap metal, touches the ship-wheel birthmark concealed on the child's chest, and draws the machines away.
- Victor Grimshaw, then in his 30s, finds Jim outside his doorway. Young Kip, age seven, watches in conspicuous striped
  pajamas. Victor takes Jim as unpaid factory labor.
- Fifteen years later, Mouse is an eighteen-year-old factory worker covered in soot, shoveling coal into a massive steam
  engine inside the hot, smoky Grimshaw Factory. Victor is now in his 50s and Kip is twenty-two.
- A separate airship crashes through the factory ceiling. Burning debris falls among the furnaces and workers. Mouse hears an
  old man trapped in the wreckage and runs toward the fire as everyone else flees.

### Episode 2 - The Puzzle Box and Blue Energy

- Mouse finds the old man pinned beneath a burning section of the crashed airship. He lifts the beam, drags the man clear,
  and remains beside him as the man's scorched clothing and shallow breathing show fatal injury.
- The old man gives Mouse a small silver puzzle box covered in gears, strange engravings, metal seams, and a ship-wheel emblem.
  Mouse recognizes that the emblem matches the birthmark beneath his dirty shirt.
- The box glows soft blue. Light travels from it along Mouse's arms to his chest, where the birthmark burns bright blue.
- Collapsing beams fall toward Mouse. He raises a hand instinctively and releases a blue energy wave that destroys the debris.
- Victor seizes and discards the box after failing to open it. When Mouse touches its emblem, gears click, the brass seams
  separate, and the box opens in his hands.

### Episode 3 - The Map Awakens

- The open puzzle box emits a low mechanical hum and projects a three-dimensional holographic map made of blue light: clouds,
  sky routes, floating islands, and a silver X marking the destination.
- Victor identifies the destination as the Floating Island of Silver and recognizes the map as a path to the lost Silver
  fortune. Greed becomes his immediate motive.
- Mouse connects the box emblem, his birthmark, and the legend but does not yet understand his identity.
- Victor refuses to let Mouse claim the map. Kip takes the box for him.
- Heavy mechanical footsteps announce the arrival of present-day Long John Silver. He is now forty-five to fifty, scarred and
  bearded, with an eyepatch, a steam-powered prosthetic leg, and an articulated brass-and-steel arm ending in a heavy claw.
  His clothes are worn from pirate life. He aims a steam-powered laser gun at Victor and demands the map.

### Episode 4 - The Uneasy Partnership

- Long John identifies the puzzle box as a map intended for him. Victor claims it as payment for the factory damage.
- Kip draws a laser pistol while Long John powers his own weapon. Victor prevents a fight and proposes a partnership:
  Long John supplies the ship, while the Grimshaws bring the map and split the treasure.
- Long John accepts and schedules departure for dawn.
- At the Steam City docks the next morning, sunlight breaks through the smog. Merchant vessels, cargo haulers, berths,
  cargo stacks, crews, travelers, and large airships create a crowded working harbor.
- Mouse sees the Storm Runner for the first time: a beautiful airborne wooden sailing ship with three masts, flowing white
  sails on wooden spars, and spinning brass propellers beneath the stern.
- Long John's claw catches Mouse by the arm as he questions the boy's role in the expedition.

### Episode 5 - Attack at the Docks

- Victor calls Mouse his property. Long John releases him but privately tells Mouse that he is more than property.
- A hostile pirate captain and roughly a dozen armed raiders emerge from behind dockside cargo stacks. They have scarred faces,
  murderous expressions, varied fighting silhouettes, and drawn swords.
- The raiders attempt to seize the Storm Runner. Long John sends Mouse aboard to warn the crew.
- Long John presses a brass control on his mechanical forearm. The claw retracts, plates rotate, gears engage, and a polished
  sword deploys from the arm in preparation for combat.

### Episode 6 - Battle on the Storm Runner

- Long John fights multiple raiders on the dock with the arm-mounted sword, combining experienced footwork, blocks, parries,
  strikes, and the strength of his mechanical limb.
- Mouse reaches the Storm Runner's main deck, where Blacktooth and Finch wait with Victor and Kip.
- Grappling hooks strike the rail and pull tight. Raiders climb aboard while Blacktooth and Finch draw swords. Victor and Kip
  retreat into the captain's cabin.
- Mad Maggie appears on deck: twenty-two, blond, beautiful and intimidating, with visible tattoos on her arms, practical pirate
  combat clothing, a sword, and a compact knife carried in a leg sheath.
- Three large raiders disarm and overpower Maggie. Mouse's concealed birthmark glows blue as his anger rises. A blue shockwave
  erupts from him and throws the attackers over the railing.

### Episode 7 - Into the Open Sky

- Maggie questions the unexplained blast. Mouse denies understanding it. Long John returns aboard and changes his mechanical
  arm from its bloodied sword form back into the claw.
- The Storm Runner climbs through Steam City's gray smog into a clear, bright blue sky. Mouse stands at the bow, exhilarated by
  open air and flight.
- Blacktooth and Finch work heavy sail ropes. Maggie carries a large coil, rejects Mouse's help, places her leg-sheath knife at
  his throat as a warning, then climbs high into the rigging.
- A violent lightning storm forms ahead. Dark clouds, heavy rain, wind, internal lightning, and repeated impacts make the ship
  roll and shudder while Long John remains at the helm.
- A loose rope catches Maggie's leg high above the deck. She falls and hangs tangled in the rigging as the masts and sails whip.
  Mouse begins climbing toward her when the rest of the crew refuses the risk.

### Episode 8 - Rigging Rescue

- Mouse climbs through rain, wind, lightning, and violently moving rigging to reach Maggie.
- Maggie gives him the knife from her leg sheath. Mouse cuts the rope trapping her, catches her after she drops, and leaves both
  of them suspended together above the main deck.
- Lightning damages the mast and rigging. The sails must be lowered before the storm tears them away.
- The release rope lies beyond a splintered break in the mast. Mouse prepares to jump while the birthmark begins glowing blue.

### Episode 9 - The Impossible Leap

- Blue energy surrounds Mouse and carries him farther than a human jump should allow. He reaches the release rope.
- Mouse releases the sails into their storm position. The canvas folds inward, strain leaves the masts, and the ship steadies.
- After Mouse and Maggie descend bruised and exhausted, Victor and Kip attack and humiliate him.
- Kip tears Mouse's dirty shirt while lifting him. A narrow portion of the ship-wheel birthmark becomes visible. Long John stops
  Kip and notices the incomplete mark but cannot see enough to identify it.

### Episode 10 - First Mate

- The storm has passed; wet rigging and rainwater remain across the deck.
- Victor blocks Long John's view and falsely claims the mark is a brand placed on all his workers.
- Long John's claw restrains Victor before he can strike Mouse again. He recognizes Mouse's courage and aptitude for skyfaring
  and offers to make him first mate.
- Victor outwardly agrees, then privately tells Kip that Mouse must remain ignorant of his identity. Both plan to kill Long John
  and Mouse after reaching the treasure.
- Long John brings Mouse to the steering deck for training. His phrase "stay alive" triggers Mouse's fragmentary memory of his
  father saying the same words before hiding him fifteen years earlier.

### Episode 11 - Sword Training

- Long John begins a dangerous training exercise on the steering deck. He gives Mouse a separate sword while transforming his
  own mechanical arm from claw to blade.
- Mouse dodges repeated attacks across the wooden deck and around the brass control structures.
- Instinct takes over during a strike toward his head. Mouse raises the sword and blocks Long John's blade inches from his face.
  The impact produces a hard steel clash and reveals skill Mouse has never been taught consciously.

### Episode 12 - Skill in the Blood

- Mouse and Long John continue dueling. Mouse repeatedly blocks and becomes nearly equal to Long John's attacks through
  inherited instinct rather than training.
- Mouse repeats Victor's lie that his father was a drunken criminal who sold him. Long John reassures him that a person can
  choose a new life rather than be defined by the past.
- Long John invites Mouse to steer the ship.
- At the controls, Mouse studies a long wooden console with levers, gears, and a mechanical compass pointing north. Maggie joins
  him; their guarded relationship softens around their shared love of freedom in the sky.
- Kip pushes Mouse down and begins harassing Maggie.

### Episode 13 - Taking the Helm

- Mouse stands between Kip and Maggie when Kip reaches for his pistol. Long John intervenes, throws Kip back, and orders him
  away from the controls.
- Maggie thanks Mouse before leaving. Long John warns him that affection for a pirate can be dangerous.
- Long John explains the steering console, the mechanical compass, and the ship's giant brass helm, which is taller than Mouse.
- Mouse places both hands on the wheel for the first time. Kip watches from a distance and decides to sabotage him.

### Episode 14 - The Near Revelation

- Mouse steers the Storm Runner through soft white clouds while Long John guides his grip and reads the ship's resistance.
- Long John recalls the son he lost. A flashback shows Silver City at night under attack by giant machines and John escaping in
  the same royal ship later seen crashing into Steam City, with young Jim in the passenger seat.
- Long John says his son carried the House of Silver crest on his chest, shaped like a ship's helm. Mouse realizes the
  description matches his birthmark and begins opening his shirt to reveal it.
- Unseen at the control panel, Kip smashes the mechanical compass with the butt of a knife. The sails shift as the ship drifts
  off course, interrupting the revelation before John sees the complete mark.

### Episode 15 - The Floating Ship Graveyard

- The Storm Runner collides with wreckage inside a vast floating graveyard: dozens of broken wooden hulls, torn sails, rusted
  freighters, and massive blimps drift at different heights and in different states of decay.
- Long John's mechanical leg becomes trapped in the deck boards. A second impact throws crew members across the deck and sends
  him over the railing.
- While falling into open sky, John changes his mechanical arm from claw into a grappling hook and fires it into the railing of
  a wrecked ship, leaving him suspended away from the Storm Runner.
- Mouse runs to the helm because no one else is steering. Victor orders him away, but Mouse refuses with new confidence.
- Maggie adds her hands to the giant wheel. Together they turn the ship just enough to avoid a wreck by inches.

### Episode 16 - The Graveyard Automaton Awakens

- Maggie calls directions from the rail while Mouse steers left and right through successive near collisions. They find an open
  route and move between wrecks without further impact.
- A colossal thirty-foot Dreadnought clings motionless to the side of an ancient rusted freighter. It is larger than the city
  units, with weathered brass armor, dark inactive eyes, and gears aged by decades of exposure.
- As the Storm Runner passes, the machine's eyes ignite red and its ancient gears grind awake. Rocket thrusters in its legs and
  back lift it into flight.
- Its shadow covers the steering deck and removes the sunlight. The automaton locks its red eyes on the Storm Runner, raises a
  giant arm that functions as a red energy cannon, and blasts the deck. Splintered wood and the explosion throw Maggie overboard.

### Episode 17 - Hunted Through the Wrecks

- Maggie lands on the deck of a separate drifting wreck. Mouse takes a rope, orders Blacktooth to hold the Storm Runner steady
  and find Long John, then jumps after her.
- The automaton magnifies Mouse in its red sensor view, recognizes him as a Silver, and redirects its attack toward him.
- Mouse and Maggie lose the return rope when an energy blast strikes the wreck.
- They hide below deck inside the broken ship. A huge shadow passes over shattered windows while red searchlights cut through
  the interior and scan across them.
- Maggie concludes that Mouse's blue power is their only effective weapon and presses him to use it, although he cannot control
  it deliberately.

### Episode 18 - The Decoy

- Maggie removes her outer pirate clothes down to her undergarments and combines her clothes with Mouse's shirt to create a
  rough human-shaped decoy at a broken window.
- The Dreadnought sees the loose clothing, marks it as the target, and turns its arm cannon toward the wreck.
- Mouse and Maggie hide close together in a confined corner while the machine passes. They emerge behind it, but Mouse cannot
  summon the blue energy.
- The automaton recognizes the decoy and turns back toward them. Maggie steadies Mouse, kisses his cheek, and gives him the
  emotional trigger to act.
- Mouse's exposed ship-wheel emblem burns bright blue. He projects a concentrated blue energy blast toward the machine.

### Episode 19 - Destruction and Betrayal

- Mouse's blue blast destroys the thirty-foot automaton in a colossal explosion.
- Mouse and Maggie dress again before returning to the Storm Runner. They describe the victory as teamwork while keeping the
  intimate trigger private.
- Kip seizes Mouse, drags him to the rail, and attempts to throw him into the open sky despite Mouse having saved the crew.

### Episode 20 - The Island Appears

- Kip releases Mouse over the side. Long John, now back aboard, catches Mouse's arm with his mechanical claw and pulls him to
  safety.
- Long John identifies the smashed compass and understands that navigation was sabotaged. He accuses Kip.
- Victor orders Mouse thrown overboard. Long John refuses, changes his mechanical arm from claw to sword, and places himself
  between Mouse and Victor. Victor draws a laser pistol, creating an armed standoff.
- Maggie interrupts and points ahead. Clouds part around an enormous Floating Island of Silver, ending the episode arc on its
  first full reveal.
- At this endpoint, the puzzle box has led them to the island, but Mouse and Long John have not confirmed that they are son and
  father. Victor and Kip remain active threats aboard the Storm Runner.

## Character and Group Continuity

### Jim Silver / Mouse

- **Age 3:** small child with blond hair, large blue eyes, and a ship-wheel birthmark centered on his chest. He is wrapped in a
  blanket during the royal-airship crash and hidden among alley scrap.
- **Age 18:** slim young factory worker with the same blond hair and blue eyes. Early episodes show soot on his skin and clothes,
  a dirty practical work shirt, and working-class trousers and footwear. The birthmark remains concealed until the shirt tears
  in Episode 9 and is fully exposed for the decoy in Episode 18.
- The birthmark glows bright blue when his inherited power activates. Demonstrated effects include a defensive blast, a wide
  shockwave, an enhanced leap, and a concentrated attack capable of destroying a giant automaton.
- Temporary states include soot and factory grime, a bleeding lip after Kip's assault, storm-soaked and bruised clothing after
  the rigging rescue, sword-training exertion, and removal of his shirt for the Episode 18 decoy.

### Long John Silver

- **Lord Jonathan Silver, 30s:** handsome and clean-cut royal heir with both natural arms and legs. During the prologue crash he
  is badly injured, bleeding from one arm and dragging one leg. He wears royal-era clothing appropriate to House of Silver.
- **Captain Long John Silver, 45-50:** thick beard, facial and body scars, one eye covered by an eyepatch, weathered pirate
  clothing, a heavy steam-powered prosthetic leg, and one articulated brass-and-steel mechanical arm. His remaining natural
  hand is distinct from the prosthetic side.
- The mechanical arm changes through visible plates, gears, pistons, and locking parts. Established modes are a heavy claw, a
  sword, and a fired grappling hook. Its transformations are mechanical, not magical. His separate steam-powered laser gun is
  carried in his natural hand when used.

### Mad Maggie, 22

- Blond, tattooed across the arms, athletic, beautiful, and visually intimidating. Her pirate clothing must support swordplay,
  climbing, rope work, and rapid movement.
- Recurring equipment includes a sword and a compact knife in a leg sheath. In Episodes 18-19 she temporarily removes her outer
  clothes to build the decoy, remains in undergarments inside the wreck, and is dressed again before returning to the ship.

### Victor Grimshaw

- **30s prologue form:** arrogant, overweight industrial figure who discovers young Jim at the alley doorway.
- **50s present-day form:** controlling factory owner whose clothing and finish show wealth and authority over workers. He uses
  physical intimidation and carries a laser pistol by Episode 20.

### Kip Grimshaw

- **Age 7:** arrogant child in conspicuous striped pajamas at Victor's doorway.
- **Age 22:** spoiled industrial heir in inherited luxury. He carries a laser pistol, later uses a knife butt to smash the
  Storm Runner's compass, and repeatedly attacks Mouse.

### Supporting Characters and Groups

- **Blacktooth:** burly, powerful pirate sailor with a heavy working silhouette, sword, and shipboard equipment.
- **Finch:** burly pirate sailor and opportunistic fighter with a silhouette and equipment distinct from Blacktooth.
- **Old man:** injured courier from the factory-crash airship; scorched clothes, shallow breathing, and fatal burns or impact
  injuries. He carries the silver puzzle box inside his coat.
- **Hostile pirate captain and raiders:** one leading captain and roughly a dozen armed pirates with scarred faces, cold eyes,
  varied builds, swords, grappling hooks, and raid-ready clothing.
- **Grimshaw factory workers:** laborers operating furnaces, steam engines, coal areas, pistons, and loading equipment in
  practical industrial clothing.
- **Steam City citizens and dock crowds:** workers, families, traders, cargo handlers, sailors, travelers, and bystanders with
  varied ages, builds, occupations, and social status.

## Key Props, Vehicles, and Machines

### Silver Puzzle Box

- Small mechanical silver object with interlocking gears, separable metal seams, strange engravings, and a ship-wheel emblem.
- It remains inert for Victor but responds to Mouse's touch and birthmark. Its opening sequence uses clicks, turning gears, a
  mechanical hum, separated seams, and blue light.
- It projects a volumetric blue map containing clouds, sky routes, floating islands, and a silver X.

### Storm Runner

- Large wooden steam-powered skyship that retains the legible structure of a sailing vessel: three wooden masts, wooden spars,
  flowing white sails, rigging, deck railings, a main deck, steering deck, captain's cabin, and brass propellers beneath the stern.
- The helm is a giant brass wheel taller than Mouse. The control area includes a long wooden console, levers, gears, and a large
  mechanical compass. The compass is intact through Episode 13 and visibly smashed from Episode 14 onward.
- Episode states include docked above the Steam City harbor, climbing through smog, storm-soaked with lowered sails and damaged
  rigging, navigating among wrecks, and deck damage from the graveyard Dreadnought's energy blast.

### Airships and Warships

- **Royal escape ship:** bright blue and white steam-powered sailing craft. In Episode 1 its sails burn before it crashes into
  Steam City. The Silver City flashback shows the same ship before the crash with John piloting and Jim seated beside him.
- **Factory-crash airship:** separate damaged delivery craft carrying the old man and puzzle box. It breaks through the Grimshaw
  Factory roof and leaves burning wreckage among the furnaces.
- **Black warships:** two smaller, faster hostile vessels descending through smoke. Hatches beneath their sterns release the
  fifteen-foot Dreadnought units.

### Dreadnought Automatons

- **Steam City units:** two fifteen-foot industrial war machines with brass armor plates, iron rivets, glowing red eyes, red
  searchlights, deep hollow voices, and steps heavy enough to shake the street.
- **Graveyard unit:** separate thirty-foot automaton weathered by decades on a rusted freighter. It has red optical sensors,
  ancient grinding gears, rocket thrusters in its legs and back, and one giant arm that functions as a red energy cannon.
- Red sensors, searchlights, and weapon energy identify machine surveillance and aggression. Mouse's inherited energy remains
  visually distinct in blue.

### Weapons and Working Equipment

- Long John's arm modes, separate laser gun, Maggie's sword and leg-sheath knife, raider swords and grappling hooks, Kip's laser
  pistol, Victor's laser pistol, coal shovels, sail ropes, and the Storm Runner's helm and compass recur as functional objects.
- Do not merge a carried weapon into a character's body unless this reference establishes it as part of Long John's prosthetic.

## Location Continuity

### Steam City

- Dense steampunk metropolis with brick smokestacks, square metal factories, large visible gears, pistons, steam pipes, smoke,
  smog, street-level industry, alleys, docks, and airship traffic overhead.
- Industrial threat uses furnace heat, dark metal, smoke, and red mechanical light. The upper sky becomes clear blue once the
  Storm Runner rises above the smog.

### Steam City Crash Site and Alley

- The Episode 1 crash site occupies a city street damaged by the royal ship: fire, broken timber, twisted metal, damaged
  buildings, and fleeing citizens. The pursuing warships and Dreadnoughts remain nearby.
- The adjacent alley contains doorways and discarded gears and scrap metal deep enough to hide young Jim.

### Grimshaw Factory

- Hot, smoky industrial interior with giant furnaces, a massive coal-fed steam engine, pistons, overhead structure, a skylight,
  loading and work areas, and many laborers.
- The factory-crash state adds a broken ceiling, embedded airship wreckage, fire, smoke, falling beams, and scattered burning
  debris. Treat this as a condition of the same factory, not a separate universal location.

### Steam City Docks

- Working aerial harbor with suspended airship berths, cargo stacks, gangplanks, merchant vessels, cargo haulers, ship crews,
  traders, travelers, and open sky above the smog. The Storm Runner waits airborne beside the boarding area.

### Storm Runner Spaces

- **Main deck:** open working deck with railings, rope paths, masts, sail controls, access to the captain's cabin, and enough
  circulation for crew work and combat.
- **Steering deck and controls:** raised control area with the giant brass helm, long wooden mechanical console, gears, levers,
  compass, view forward through open sky, and access back toward the main deck.
- **Rigging:** high rope network, masts, spars, and sails above the deck. During the storm it is wet, violently moving, partially
  broken, and exposed to lightning.
- **Captain's cabin:** enclosed refuge reached from the main deck; Victor and Kip hide there during the boarding attack.

### Silver City Flashback

- Royal city seen at night during machine attack. Silver streets, royal skyfaring identity, fleeing inhabitants, destructive
  giant robots, and the royal escape ship define the sequence. Do not invent architecture beyond what a specific brief needs.

### Floating Ship Graveyard

- Vast three-dimensional field of wrecked skyships at multiple heights: broken hulls, torn sails, rusted freighters, drifting
  blimps, floating debris, and open cloud-filled sky between dangerous narrow routes.
- The wreck used by Mouse and Maggie has an exposed deck, broken windows, damaged below-deck interior, confined hiding corners,
  and enough loose clothing support or broken framing to stage the decoy.

### Floating Island of Silver

- First appears at the end of Episode 20 as an enormous floating island emerging through a wall of clouds directly ahead of the
  Storm Runner. Its scale and aerial geography are established; detailed architecture, palette, terrain, and structures are not
  yet defined by Episodes 1-20 and must not be invented without another brief or approved design.

## Visual Systems and Continuity Rules

- House of Silver identity uses the ship-wheel crest, royal blue-and-white airship treatment, silver mechanisms, maintained
  skyfaring craft, and inherited blue energy.
- Grimshaw and hostile machine power uses smoke, brick, dark practical metal, furnace heat, red sensors, red searchlights, and
  red weapon energy.
- Blue Silver energy is luminous and supernatural. Steam machinery remains materially constructed from timber, iron, brass,
  steel, gears, pistons, pipes, rope, sails, and mechanical joints.
- Mouse and Long John do not know they are son and father by the Episode 20 endpoint. Do not visually present a confirmed family
  reunion before a later story source establishes it.
- Mouse's working-class identity remains visible through Episode 20. Do not promote him into royal costume merely because the
  audience knows his heritage.
- Apply temporary dirt, damage, wetness, torn clothing, exposed marks, undress, damaged machinery, or changed location state only
  when the requested episode or scene requires it. Keep permanent identity separate from episode-specific condition.
